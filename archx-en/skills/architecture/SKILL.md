# ArchX

ArchX serves its professional tools and its way of working live from the installed app (MCP server `archx`). What ArchX can do today and how it works therefore comes from the server, not from this file, and it follows the office's country in ArchX: a practice in the United Kingdom gets the British tools and rules, a firm in the United States the U.S. ones.

1. Call `archx_anleitung` with the topic `general` and with the matching topic, then work as it says. The tool lists the topics for this office itself, for example `planning`, `tenders`, `contract`, `plans`, `cad` and `model3d` (United Kingdom) or `us_code`, `zoning`, `us_project`, `us_contract`, `plans` and `proposal` (United States), plus `help` and `knowledge`. Where a topic refers to guidance such as `knowledge_guidance` or `cad_guidance`, load it.
2. Project tools need `projectRoot`: the absolute path of the project folder, usually the current working directory.
3. The tools read, measure, calculate and write the files; you make the professional decisions and give your reasons, with the instrument, section and edition of every rule you apply and your assumptions marked. Use the country's own rules: in England the GPDO, the Building Regulations and the Approved Documents, NRM and JCT; in the United States the codes the authority having jurisdiction adopted, the local zoning ordinance and the contract the firm signed.
4. If a tool reports that ArchX is not running, the license is missing or sign-in is needed, pass the wording on to the user and do not replace the tool with your own calculations.
